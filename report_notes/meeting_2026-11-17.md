## Clarified with Jo (2026-11-17)
- **1 % SIC → ocean:** water tie-point mask uses **siconc ≤ 0.01** instead of siconc = 0 (part of the tie-point selection, A4/A6b). "Limit 0.1" belongs to the same remark, not to the land mask → not relevant.
- **Ice mask, raise T_B (notes 9):** the ice tie-point mask (siconc > 0.8, **100 K** < T_B < 274 K, both hemispheres) picks up clear-ocean pixels (ocean T_B ≈ 145 K). Raise the **lower T_B limit of the ice mask**.
- **L study (note 4):** two variants, compared on the corrected SIC:
  - (a) iteration 1 with mean L, iteration 2 with **actual (per-pixel) L**
  - (b) iteration 1 with mean L, iteration 2 with **mean L** (current method)
  - Expected: (b) better; per-pixel L over-corrects (consistent with A6: per-pixel L std 11.5 % N / 13.7 % S).
- **Inverse grey Task 2 plot:** grey should mark the input range that is not really used (outside the observed 5–95 % range), white the used range. Done in B1d/B1e (confirmed by Jo).
- **Land mask:** currently **no explicit land mask**. Land drops out implicitly: the masks need `sst > 278` (water) or `siconc > 0.8` (ice), and ERA5 `sst`/`siconc` are NaN over land. The swath files also contain ERA5 `lsm` (land-sea fraction), unused so far. The provided EASE-grid mask (`provided/resampling_example/lmask_*_ease_250.nc`, codes 0/2/3) is only for the resampling example.
