## Speed-up (same day)

- Profiling `correct_day` (one day, 12 orbits): 4.6 s, of which ≈ 80 % was `ncread` reading all attributes of the
  file on every call. New `ncfast.m` (low-level netcdf, keeps the file open) gives identical arrays (checked for
  all numeric variables) and 1.1 s per day.
- `rerun_all.m` runs stage 1 (A4, A6b) and then 8 groups of independent scripts as parallel `matlab -batch`
  processes (no Parallel Computing Toolbox installed). Starts are 4 s apart and a group whose MATLAB crashes
  (seen once at startup with 8 instances) is restarted once. Logs: `logs/rerun_<group>.log`.
- Full recompute incl. globe: **≈ 25 min serial → 3.8 min parallel**. Longest group: `globe_html` (3.1 min).
  All CSVs of the parallel run are byte-identical to the serial run (C2 differs only by the C2 fix below).

## C2 fix: gaps along the ice edge and the coasts (same day)

On the globe (Feb 1973 overlay) dark cells ran along the Arctic ice edge, along the coasts and through a 76 % band in the
Weddell Sea pack. Causes: the blurred printed outlines were classified as land, the book land mask is coarser than ERA5,
and the South class-76 coast rule also hit the pack. Fix 4 in `C2_feb1973_books.md`: book land is not used where our data
is ocean (wider window at the coast), the class-76 rule only near our coast. Fix 5: `max(NaN, 0)` = 0 made our no-data cells
0 % SIC (open water); NaN is now kept.
