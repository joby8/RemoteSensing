### 3. Discussion

<mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">In</mark> conclusion<mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">,</mark> the model and the data <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">agree</mark>. <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">Over water, V and W have the biggest effect (V first in the model and in the Antarctic data, W slightly ahead in the Arctic data); Ts has almost no effect, while Ti matters over ice.</mark> <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">The</mark> direct view of the data in <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">Figures 3 and 4</mark> <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">confirms</mark> the sizes of these effects.

<mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">The</mark> remaining noise source in the correction is caused by L. In the model<mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">,</mark> L is larger than V<mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">,</mark> but in the real data it is less than half as large <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">(Figure 3)</mark>. <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">It</mark> is not corrected because the <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">ERA5 clouds</mark> do not match the 1973 scenes per pixel<mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">;</mark> the per<mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">-</mark>pixel L makes the correction worse.

<mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">The</mark> SST Ts is <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">unusable</mark> with only one 19 <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">GHz</mark> channel. <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">For</mark> a good <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">SST</mark> correction we would need another<mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">,</mark> lower frequency.

<mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">The</mark> ice temperature <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">Ti</mark> <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">gives</mark> some improvement on the ice side in the <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">Arctic: leaving it out increases the std by 1.51 %</mark>.

<mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">The</mark> <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">Antarctic</mark> summer with melting ice, wet snow and water vapour over the ice edge is a complex problem in this data. <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">Therefore</mark> the ice data in <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">Antarctica</mark> should not be <mark data-color="rgb(182, 242, 182)" style="background-color: rgb(182, 242, 182); color: inherit;">overinterpreted, since</mark> the ice noise is around 24 %.
